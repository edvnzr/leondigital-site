Roadmap

Where Doc Gen is today and what comes next.

  • Core appDone

    Quotations, invoices, receipts, credit, debit and refund notes; payments; PDF sharing; backup and restore; Malaysian SST, SSM and TIN fields.

  • Accounts and upgradesDone

    Optional sign-in, Remove Ads, premium document designs, payment reminders and bank or DuitNow details on invoices.

  • Closed testingNow

    Testers use Doc Gen for two weeks and tell us what to improve.

  • Public release on Google PlayNext

    Doc Gen for everyone on Android, free with ads.

  • Doc Gen ProPlanned

    A subscription with multiple businesses, cloud backup and sync between phones, sales and tax reports, recurring invoices and your own document designs.

  • Web appPlanned

    Use Doc Gen in your browser, with the same documents as your phone (with Pro sync).

  • iPhonePlanned

    Doc Gen on iOS, with your account, purchases and documents.

Plans can change. Tell us what matters most to you on the feedback page.
